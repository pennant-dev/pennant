# Pennant 0.2.0

One place to talk to Pennant, and Pennant working beside you instead of on your screen.

- **The Pennant chat.** You talk to Pennant in one chat. It answers what it can right away and starts threads for the rest. The threads run on their own, and Pennant tells you what came of them in its own words. Threads are there to read; you talk to Pennant. Their questions come to you as Pennant's questions, and their cards come as cards in the chat.
- **A heartbeat.** Every 30 minutes, or what you choose in Settings › Pennant, Pennant looks over its work by itself. It starts the goal sessions that are due, so goals no longer need schedules of their own. Work that's stuck or has waited on you too long gets dealt with or brought to you. A look with nothing in it costs nothing.
- **Pennant in your Chrome.** Pennant's extension lets it work in tabs of its own in your Chrome, with your sign-ins. It uses its own window and a tab group, and Chrome's own input, so your pointer, keyboard and tabs stay yours.
  - **Setup:** Settings › Pennant › Chrome › **Set it up for me** adds the extension. It updates itself when Pennant does.
  - **Sign-off:** sending, publishing, deleting and paying still wait for your OK. A card before each new site is a switch on the same page.
- **Working beside you.**
  - Pennant has its own cursor, drawn above your windows, so you can see where it's working.
  - It works in your Mac apps in the background: it sees a window even when others cover it, and presses, types and scrolls there without your pointer.
  - It borrows your real pointer and screen only when nothing else will do. It still lets go the moment you touch the mouse.
- **Steadier threads:**
  - A thread stops at a sensible size and reports what it has.
  - A thread at its limit collects its helpers instead of stopping them halfway, and helpers answer to their thread instead of waiting on you.
  - Stopped work takes its browser scripts with it.
  - One card per decision, even across threads.
- **The sidebar on the Mac:** the Pennant chat sits big and right above the work, quiet threads fade back, and goals have a finish flag.
- **The iPhone:**
  - The app opens on the Pennant chat, with threads behind a button in its title bar.
  - The live screen has multi-touch, zoom you can move around, a trackpad mode, and a keyboard that types straight onto the Mac.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - ChatGPT accounts list the account's own models.
  - Signing in from the iPhone works again.
  - `pennant memory forget` reaches standing instructions.
  - The Dashboard is ⌘1 (⌘0 is View › Actual Size again).
  - `pennant chrome` and `pennant tool` are new on the command line.

Requires macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. The Chrome extension needs Google Chrome. This is a preview; expect rough edges, and please report them.
