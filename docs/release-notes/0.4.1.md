# Pennant 0.4.1

Reports come as cards now, and the cards waiting for your OK look like what they'll send.

- **Reports as cards.** When Pennant has numbers, a list of things with their times or states, or a comparison for you, it shows them as a card: figures with their trends, bar, line and pie charts, tables, tabs, callouts and timelines, in Pennant's colours, light and dark. The cards are built from [json-render](https://github.com/vercel-labs/json-render)'s components.
- **Buttons you tap to answer.** A card's buttons send their words as your reply, or open a link.
- **The chat shows cards too.** A thread's card comes up in the chat with its news, and the chat makes one itself when something is easier to see than read.
- **Light on memory.** Each card is a picture until you point at it (on the iPhone, until you tap it), so a chat full of cards stays as light as a chat without them.
- **Everywhere else, its words.** Copy, Telegram and iMessage get the card as text. Teams gets it as a Teams card, with its facts, table and reply buttons.
- **Cards waiting for your OK look like what they'll send.** An email shows its subject, From, To and Cc. A post shows the account it goes out on and the platform's character limit. A message shows as a bubble, and a command as a terminal line with "Approve & run".

Requires macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. This is a preview; expect rough edges, and please report them.
